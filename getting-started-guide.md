# Getting Started with PropHub SaaS

Welcome to PropHub! This guide walks you through setting up your agency workspace, connecting your regional Multi-Listing Service (MLS) account, and inviting your real estate team members.

Follow these initial steps to ensure that your property listings sync automatically across your mobile and web applications.

---

## Before You Begin

Before you begin the onboarding process, ensure you have:
* An active **PropHub Agency Administrator account** login credentials.
* Your 10-digit **Regional MLS Board Broker ID**.
* A digital copy of your agency's logo (PNG or JPEG format, max 5MB).

---

## Step 1: Completing Your Agency Profile

The first time you log in, you must configure your core company details to brand your automated client reports.

1. Choose **Settings** > **Agency Profile** in the left-hand navigation sidebar.
2. Enter your legal brokerage name in the **Organization Name** field.
3. Click **Upload Logo** and select your agency's logo file.
4. Verify your local currency and time zone configurations match your physical location.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

> ⚠️ **Important:** You cannot edit your organization name and logo once your MLS feed goes live. Double-check all spellings before saving.

---

## Step 2: Connecting Your Regional MLS Feed

PropHub uses an automated connection framework to stream local market property data directly into your account database.

1. On the settings dashboard, click the **Integrations** tab.
2. Locate the **MLS Data Connections** module and click **Connect New Feed**.
3. Select your local real estate board from the dropdown directory list.
4. Input your 10-digit **Broker ID** and password credentials provided by your regional board.
5. Click **Authorize Connection**.

The initial data sync takes approximately **15 to 30 minutes** to compile your current active inventory listings.

---

## Step 3: Adding Real Estate Agents

Now that you have configured your workspace, add your active agents so that they can begin tracking client property views.

1. Select the **Team Management** panel.
2. Click the blue **Invite Member** button in the upper right corner.
3. Enter the email address of the agent you wish to invite.
4. Assign their workspace access level using the **Role** dropdown menu:
   * **Agent:** Can create client sheets, view properties, and run reports.
   * **Manager:** Can edit listings, adjust billing details, and manage team seats.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

Your agent will receive an automated email containing a secure link to create their individual profile password.

---

## Troubleshooting Common Setup Issues

### The MLS Feed connection button is greyed out
* **Cause:** Your account lacks global administrator privileges, or you haven't uploaded an organization logo yet.
* **Solution:** Return to Step 1 and verify your logo is fully saved. If the issue persists, contact your agency’s account owner.

### Invited agents are not appearing in the team directory
* **Cause:** The invitation link expires automatically after 48 hours for data security.
* **Solution:** Click the three dots `...` next to the agent's name in your directory and select **Resend Activation Email**.
