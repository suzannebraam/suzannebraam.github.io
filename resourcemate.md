# Reserving a Library Book from the Church Website

Did you know that the library catalogue is available online?<br>

### 1.  Navigate to the church website.<br>
<br>
   
<img src="./images/homepage.png" alt="LRC Church Website" width="573">
   
### 2.  Choose "Library" from the Resources menu.<br>
<br>
<img src="./images/Chooselibrary.png" alt="Choose Library" width="573">
   
### 3.  The library catalogue appears:
<br>
 <img src="./images/Catalogue.png" alt="Library Catalogue" width="573">

 - We have over 3000 books in our collection
 - The newest titles appear at the bottom of the screen
  
### 4.  Search for and select a book.<br>

 <img src="./images/Catalogue.png" alt="Library Catalogue" width="573">

   You can search by Title, Author, or Keyword.
4.  Add your book to the book bin.
5.  Mail your selection to library@lrc.church
