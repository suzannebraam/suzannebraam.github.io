# Tribute to My Mom

Good morning. <br>
On behalf of our family, we want to thank you for coming to celebrate Mom’s life with us and for all the support you showed us during this difficult month. 
If you were here for my Dad’s service, you heard about what a hard worker he was, but what you may not realize is that Mom was very busy too. 
Just like in the poem we read, Mom’s hands were always busy.<br> _Mom had working hands, mending hands, and praying hands_. 

## First, Mom had working hands.

### She was always busy.

She kept the house spotless and she made everything from scratch.<br>
None of us had ever seen a TV dinner until it was served at a friend’s house.<br>
Mom made very traditional German and Mennonite food. We had Zwieback, Cabbage Rolls, Borscht, and Bonnensuppe.<br>

She kept us well fed on a tight budget. 

### Nothing was ever wasted. 

Leftovers were made into the next day’s meal.<br> 
The turkey carcass and leftover meat were made into soup.

### When we were in Bamberg, Mom and Dad grew their own vegetables and raised their own meat.

Mom had a green thumb so we always had mountains of vegetables. <br>
Everything that wasn’t eaten raw was chopped and blanched, frozen, pickled, canned, or made into jam. <br>
The ones that weren’t fit for consumption were fed to the animals or put in the compost to feed next year’s crop of vegetables.   

### Mom was an avid baker as well. 

Every holiday, in fact, **_every season_** involved baking.<br> 
Summer meant strawberry shortcake.<br>
Easter meant paska.<br>
Christmas meant sprinkle cookies, and <br>
Birthdays meant chocolate cake.

On the morning of our birthday, Mom would get up early and wake us up by singing "Happy Birthday" holding a cake and candles.<br>
We got to blow out the candles and open our present before we went to school, and then we ate chocolate cake for breakfast.

### But the baking we looked forward to most was Mom’s apple pie, which she made for _every_ occasion. 

Mom was very artistic. And whenever she made a pie, instead of just piercing the steam vents, she would draw a picture in the dough.<br> 
Mostly it was a branch with flowers, but at Easter it would be a cross, or at Christmas it would be a tree, candle, or wreath.<br> 

### _Each pie was a work of art, created with love from her hard-working hands_. 

## Second, Mom had mending hands. 

In addition to being a skilled cook, she was very crafty.<br> 
She knitted, crocheted, painted, and sewed.<br>

### Because Mom’s first language was German, she struggled with reading, so she didn’t really read patterns.

She could just look at something and figure out how it was made.<br>
And many times she could modify and improve on how it was made.

### When Maggie was little, she had a friend named Lynn who had a cute store-bought vest. 

Mom asked Lynn’s mother if she could borrow the vest.<br>
Mom took it home, figured out how it was made, and created her own vest for Maggie that was even nicer than Lynn’s vest. 

### Just as each holiday had its own special food, Mom made special clothes for special occasions, such as Halloween costumes.

One year, Mom designed a bear costume out of furry material that was so warm you didn’t have to wear a coat.<br>
She sewed many matching outfits and Confirmation dresses for Irma and Linda when they were little girls.<br>
She made a prom dress for Betty. <br>
She made a flower girl dress for Maggie to wear at Irma’s wedding.<br> 
She made an Easter dress for me.<br>

She let out waistbands, patched knees, darned socks, shortened sleeves, and hemmed countless pairs of pants.<br>
  
### And just as Mom was a mender of clothes, she was also a mender of relationships. 

Mom was the peacemaker of the family.<br>
When personalities would clash, Mom was the one who picked up the pieces and sewed them back together. 

### Mom once crocheted a tablecloth that had a complicated pattern sized for a table much larger than hers. 

Mom was able to adapt the difficult pattern so that it fit her smaller table perfectly.<br>
In the same way, Mom was the one who was adaptable. <br>

### _She made the adjustments that were needed to accommodate her environment: She had mending hands_.  

## And finally, Mom had praying hands. 

### Mom loved Jesus and she believed in the power of prayer.

God had protected her from the soldiers when she was a little girl.<br>
God had allowed her family to escape during the war.<br>
Mom grew up knowing that God was faithful and she taught us to pray.<br>

 _Mom prayed with us and Mom prayed for us and Mom prayed over us_.   

### Mom loved flowers and she could make anything grow. 

She could rescue any plant that was on the verge of death and she could make it bloom.<br>
My parents used to live across the street from me and when I would go on vacation, she would often look after my hanging pots and flowerbeds when we went away.<br>

Now I have a black thumb, and usually by the time we left for vacation, my hanging pots were just about ready to be thrown out.<br>
We would return from vacation after one or two week’s time, and my baskets were overflowing with blooms, and they looked _**nicer**_ than they did when I bought them.<br> 

_In the same way, Mom’s joy and her personality and her love of the Lord would just over flow_.

### Mom was very musical and she loved to sing. 

Her favourite song was “_Victory in Jesus_”. <br>
Mom painted a beautiful picture of Jesus that has been hanging over her bed for as long as I can remember.<br>

Mom also loved angels. <br>
We always had an angel instead of a star on top of our Christmas tree, and she had a small collection of angel figurines.<br>

### Just before she was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s, Mom had a vision of an angel standing in her bedroom. 

As her illness progressed, Mom found it harder and harder to communicate. <br>
She could barely speak, but she could still pray and she could still sing those hymns.<br>

### And although it was so hard to see her slip away, her illness in some ways was a blessing. 

Her illness shielded her from the pain of Dad’s death.<br>
Her illness erased all the worries she carried.<br>
She became happy, silly, and so much fun to hang out with.<br>

She would greet us all like this: (jazz hands wave) 

She made everyone feel so special.<br>
She brought joy to others.<br>

_From the Jesus picture hanging in her room to the fact that mom bowed her head and prayed before meals, people could tell that she loved Jesus and they wanted to be around her_.

### Mom brought the joy of the Lord to others. 

The day before Dad passed away, we were praying in Dad’s room when Mom looked up and said, “I see angels\!” <br>
We asked where and she pointed to Dad’s head and Dad’s feet and she suddenly understood that Dad was going to be with Jesus. <br>

God loved mom so much....

### _He let the woman who loved angels see angels escorting her husband home. Mom had praying hands_.  

## In Closing

Since it is so close to Christmas, and since we are here at WK church, I wanted to share our favourite Christmas memory. 

We grew up in this church, and we were always part of the Christmas program on Christmas Eve. <br>
So, we would get ready and then we’d all get in the car and wait for Mom.<br>

### And Mom was always about 10 minutes late.

And we’d sit in the car on the driveway and Dad would start to get impatient.<br>
He’d adjust his seat, fiddle with the heat, and give a big sigh.<br>
He’d honk the horn, he’d keep checking his watch, and he'd grumble: 

### _“Oh, come on Frieda\! What’s taking so long?_”

Finally Mom would get in the car and we’d go to church <br>
and when we got home, we found that Santa had come\! 

### The tree that was empty when we left was now overflowing when we got back\! 

And I was always so amazed that Santa knew that our family was German, and that we opened our presents at night, <br>
so he made sure to come to our house early while we were at church!

_And I didn’t clue in that reason why Mom was always late_ <br>
_was because she was busy putting all the presents under the tree_...<br>

#### ..._and that Dad was just pretending to be annoyed_. 

So, although Mom's passing happened so soon after Dad passed away, <br>
we can just picture Dad in heaven, <br>
and after waiting exactly one month to the day, <br>
he looked at his watch, sighed, and said:

### _“Oh, come on Frieda\! What’s taking so long?_”
